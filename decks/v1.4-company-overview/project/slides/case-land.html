Value Through Patience

Commercial land, Inland Empire, California

54-acre commercial site

$12.7 million

Entirely with equity

Situation

Land earns no income while its value matures, so owners who borrow against a site often must sell on a lender’s schedule. A 54-acre commercial site in the Inland Empire, with freeway frontage and retail approvals, was available at $5.39 per square foot against a comparable sale above $31.

Action

REALM acquired the site with an operating partner in February 2024 for $12.7 million, entirely with equity, and has carried the land without debt since. REALM kept the existing approvals in place to preserve the widest pool of buyers while two large developments nearby add roads and demand around the site.

Value created

With no loan to repay, REALM sets the timing of the sale. The venture has returned $1.3 million of reserves to investors, and REALM rates the asset on track against an original underwriting that assumes a sale in 2028 at roughly $25 per square foot (projected).
