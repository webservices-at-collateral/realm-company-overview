Value Through Judgment

An acquisition REALM declined, San Jose

One-fifth of the cost to build

$43 million of equity

Situation

A downtown San Jose office campus was offered in a distressed sale at roughly one-fifth of the cost to build. The largest tenant held a quarter of the space and had not signed a lease renewal. Other buyers were bidding, and the seller wanted deposits made non-refundable before the tenant signed.

Action

REALM made the renewal a condition of its deposit becoming non-refundable, held to that condition through the process, and withdrew when the seller declined. REALM then told its investors what happened and why.

Value created

REALM kept roughly $43 million of equity out of an investment that depended on a renewal the tenant had not signed. Another buyer closed the transaction, and REALM’s investors responded positively when REALM explained the decision.
