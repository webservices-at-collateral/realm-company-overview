Questions REALM asks

Timing

Real estate is illiquid, so market prices can lead or lag a dislocation.

Is the dislocation early, middle, or late in the cycle, and where is pricing? When will each correct?

Valuation

Prices often overreact to a dislocation, opening buying opportunities.

How far have prices moved? Do they fully reflect the dislocation? Are high-quality assets trading below intrinsic value?

Opportunity

Dislocation, cycle timing, price against value, and proprietary sourcing together produce favorable risk-adjusted returns.

Which markets and property types have overcorrected relative to a temporary dislocation? Where does REALM have an edge? Which position in the capital stack fits the dislocation?
