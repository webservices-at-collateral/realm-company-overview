Value Through Advice

A third-generation family

Situation

Families who built their wealth in an operating business often take on real estate projects of their own without a team to evaluate them. A third-generation family in the energy business, with little experience in real estate, began pursuing a townhome development and then an undeveloped retail site.

Action

REALM worked as an extension of the family office, advising on structure, environmental studies, site access, and diligence for each project.

Value created

The family gained the judgment of an experienced real estate team on projects outside its own expertise, without building that team in-house. The family continues to bring new projects to REALM, and the relationship now extends from the investments REALM offers to the real estate the family develops on its own.
