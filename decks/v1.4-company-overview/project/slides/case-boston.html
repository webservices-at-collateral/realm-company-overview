Value Through Asset Management

Advanced manufacturing facility, outside Boston

$9.0 million

8.5% going-in cap rate

Situation

A building with a below-market lease near expiry sells at a discount, because buyers price in the risk of a vacancy. A 54,000-square-foot advanced manufacturing facility outside Boston, home to the same government-affiliated tenant since 2007, carried such a lease, expiring in 2027.

Action

REALM acquired the property off-market in June 2024 for $9.0 million, entirely with equity, at an 8.5% going-in cap rate, and opened renewal discussions with the tenant well ahead of the expiry.

Value created

The renewal rents under discussion are 40% above underwriting, and REALM has raised its exit assumption from $249 to $280 per square foot (projected).

Figures are as of August 2026 unless dated otherwise, and forward-looking figures are marked as projected. Photograph is illustrative, not the property.
