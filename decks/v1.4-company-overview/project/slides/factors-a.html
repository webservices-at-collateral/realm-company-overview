Factor

What the factor reads

Questions REALM asks

Dislocation

A sharp break in value from a loss of demand, oversupply, or a shift in capital markets.

What is causing the dislocation? Is the dislocation structural or temporary? How severe is the break, and what would correct the market?

Demand

Real estate is a secondary industry: its value comes from demand in the primary industries that occupy the space.

Where is primary-industry demand growing? Is demand accelerating or slowing, what drives the growth, and how durable is that demand?

Supply

Supply of physical space often follows liquidity more than fundamental demand.

How much supply exists, and how much is available or obsolete? How much is coming, and when? Is demand or liquidity driving new supply?

Capital markets

Real estate is a levered asset class, highly sensitive to liquidity and interest rates.

Which way are rates and liquidity moving, and how fast? When does the market’s outstanding debt mature? Will maturities or tight liquidity force discounted sales or recapitalizations?
