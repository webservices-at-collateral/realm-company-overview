Advice: The Family’s Real Estate

REALM works with each family to construct its real estate strategy, alongside the chief investment officer and the family’s other advisers.

The advice comes from an investor that owns and manages real estate in the same markets, and a family can ask REALM to review its holdings with no transaction attached.

The role of real estate

What real estate should do in the family’s portfolio, whether durable income, diversification away from a concentrated equity position, tax deferral, or growth, and the mix of strategies, holding periods, and structures that delivers that role.

Portfolio review

An asset-by-asset view of the real estate the family already owns, with a recommendation on what to hold, sell, or exchange.

Exchange planning

Timing a sale, identifying replacement property within the exchange deadlines, and modeling what a chain of exchanges adds over time.

A second opinion

REALM underwrites an opportunity the family has been shown or has sourced, with the same process REALM applies to its own investments.

Inherited portfolios

A review for heirs taking on the family’s buildings, with a plan for each asset.
