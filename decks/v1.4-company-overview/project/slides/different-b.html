What Makes Family Capital Different

Real estate investment for families should begin from those priorities and hold to them through every decision.

For a family

How REALM answers

Transparency and clear reporting

Families expect to see how their capital is performing, in plain terms.

Clear, consistent reporting on each investment, with candid updates when plans change.

Relationships first

Families invest with people they know, often on the word of a family they respect.

Direct access to REALM’s team, and a network of families built largely through introductions.

Legacy beyond money

Families judge success by what passes to the next generation, in values as well as capital.

A place for the next generation in the family’s real estate decisions, and gatherings that bring generations together.

Liquidity that matters

Families want to know when and how their capital returns.

Investments chosen with each family’s liquidity needs in view, and exits timed to the market.

Customization

Every family’s holdings, tax position, and goals are its own.

A real estate allocation constructed with each family from strategies across the risk-return spectrum.
