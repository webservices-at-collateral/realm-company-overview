Value Through Basis

4696 Willow, Pleasanton, California

$8.5 million

$199 per square foot

One-quarter of replacement cost

Situation

Buyers had stepped away from life science real estate, and even a well-equipped vacant building drew little competition. A 42,620-square-foot biomanufacturing facility in Pleasanton came to market empty but move-in ready, with more than $14 million of specialized improvements in place.

Action

REALM acquired the property with an operating partner in September 2025 for $8.5 million, or $199 per square foot, roughly one-quarter of replacement cost, with the seller financing half of the price, and began marketing the building for lease the following quarter.

Value created

The low basis lets REALM offer a turnkey facility on terms a new building cannot match.

Figures are as of August 2026 unless dated otherwise, and forward-looking figures are marked as projected. Photograph is illustrative, not the property.
