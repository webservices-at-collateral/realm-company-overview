Value Through Dislocation

CitySpire, Midtown Manhattan

$130 million

8.5% in-place cap rate

99% leased

Situation

Institutional capital had left Manhattan office even as Midtown leasing recovered, leaving well-leased buildings priced for distress. One of them, a 24-floor Class A office condominium three blocks from Central Park, 99% leased, was held in a complex REIT structure that had to be unwound.

Action

REALM acquired the 377,452-square-foot property with an operating partner in May 2026 for $130 million, or $344 per square foot, and unwound the REIT structure at closing, with REALM’s families funding the full equity requirement.

Value created

Working through the REIT structure allowed REALM to acquire at an 8.5% in-place cap rate, against 6.0% to 6.5% for comparable Class A Midtown properties, and at a total basis of $377 per square foot against $389 underwritten. Early leasing and renewals have run 16% above underwriting, the projected 2026 cash-on-cash yield is 9.1%, and REALM rates the asset out-performing.

Figures are as of August 2026 unless dated otherwise, and forward-looking figures are marked as projected. Photograph is illustrative, not the property.
