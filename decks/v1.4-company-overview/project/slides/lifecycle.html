Lifecycle Management

REALM manages each investment from acquisition to exit as a hands-on owner, alongside its operating partners.

One team is accountable for each investment from acquisition to exit, so decisions on leasing, financing, and the timing of a sale are made with the family’s objectives and tax position in view.

Asset management

REALM oversees each business plan with its operating partners, including leasing, capital projects, and financing, and acts early when an asset falls behind.

Reporting

Clear reporting on each asset against its original business plan, consolidated across the family’s entities, with a dedicated update and a stated plan for any asset that falls behind.

Exit and reinvestment

Each sale timed to the market and planned around the family’s tax position, including exchanges into the next REALM investment.
