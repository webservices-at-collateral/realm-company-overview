REALM Serves Families First

Each relationship starts from the family’s own position: its holdings, its tax position, and the generation that will inherit.

Founder-led families

Principals who built their wealth through an operating business or real estate and want real estate that compounds on their terms.

Single-family offices

Offices with lean teams and a generalist chief investment officer that need a partner to source, structure, and manage the real estate they cannot staff for.

Multi-generational families

Families preparing to pass wealth, and real estate in particular, to heirs who need to understand what they will own.

Families after a liquidity event

Families placing proceeds from the sale of a business or a property, often against an exchange deadline or a tax event.

Multi-family offices

Offices serving several families that want a real estate specialist built around the same priorities they serve.

Beyond the investor

Operating partners

The local operators REALM invests with, who bring opportunities and expertise and return for the next deal.

Advisers

The accountants, attorneys, and wealth managers who introduce families and rely on REALM to put those families first.

The REALM team

Analysts, family office advisers, and investment professionals, several introduced by REALM’s own investors, who carry the relationship day to day.
