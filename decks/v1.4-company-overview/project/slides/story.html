REALM began with a conviction.

Family Capital deserves real estate investment built intentionally for what families value.

REALM’s founders came to that conviction as owners.

In 2019 they built REALM to invest the way their own families wanted to invest.

One introduction led to the next. Today REALM partners with the nation’s most successful families, the great majority introduced by a family they already knew, and many now bring the next generation to the table.

The founding conviction still sets REALM’s standard: families come first in every structure, every report, and every decision about what to buy and when to sell, and every family that joins strengthens the whole.
