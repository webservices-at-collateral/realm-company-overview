Content runs from y 72 to y 960. The footer is one 30 px row inside the bottom margin, from y 1006 to y 1036 (yellow): logo, section name, page number, and on pages 21 to 25 the service stepper.

Against the CP standard of 50 pt on every side, this gives each slide 24 px more width and 28 px more height, on top of the space won back from v1.3’s eyebrow and tall footer.
