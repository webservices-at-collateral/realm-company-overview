Value Through Aggregation

Manufactured housing, North Carolina

11 communities

649 lots

$42,300 per lot

Situation

Manufactured housing is owned one community at a time: ownership is fragmented, few sales are brokered, and the country adds roughly 1,700 new lots a year against 420,000 apartments. Institutional buyers pay a premium for portfolios already assembled and stabilized.

Action

REALM entered the sector in October 2024 through a programmatic joint venture with a local operating partner, sourcing communities ahead of competitive bidding and applying one plan to each: move lot rents to market, sell park-owned homes to residents, and fill vacant lots.

Value created

The venture owns 11 communities and 649 lots at a basis of $42,300 per lot, against an average of $83,375 per lot in recent comparable sales (September 2026). In the first portfolio, in Raleigh, lot rents have risen 11%, 39 homes have been sold to residents against 23 underwritten, and an appraisal of $12.3 million against a $9.4 million basis supported a refinancing after 18 months, more than two years ahead of plan. Net operating income stands at 97% of underwriting.

Figures are as of August 2026 unless dated otherwise, and forward-looking figures are marked as projected. Photograph is illustrative, not the property.
