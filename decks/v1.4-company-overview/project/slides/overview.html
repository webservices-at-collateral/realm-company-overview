A multi-strategy real estate investment manager built intentionally for Family Capital.

Families

The nation’s most successful families, investing through single-family offices, multi-family offices, and directly as principals.

How REALM serves

REALM gives each family Access, Advice, and Actionable Intel: proprietary opportunities, guidance on the family’s real estate, and ground-level market insight.

Strategies

Strategies across the risk-return spectrum and the capital stack, from durable, tax-efficient income to complex, distressed, and forced-sale opportunities, with credit, GP, and secondaries to follow.

Process

Each relationship follows five steps: understand the family, construct its allocation, select through the REALM Factor Framework, manage through the full lifecycle, and share what REALM learns.
