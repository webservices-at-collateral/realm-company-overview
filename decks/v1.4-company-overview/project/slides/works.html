How REALM Works

Each relationship follows the same arc: REALM understands the family, constructs its strategy, selects each investment through the REALM Factor Framework, manages each investment through its full lifecycle, and shares what REALM learns with its families.

Usually through an introduction from another family. REALM learns the family’s portfolio, tax position, and goals for the next generation.

REALM and the family set the role of real estate, the strategies that fit, and the structures that suit the family’s tax position.

Off-market opportunities pass through the REALM Factor Framework and asset-level diligence before a business plan is set.

REALM and its operating partners execute the plan, report each asset against its underwriting, and time each exit.

What REALM learns returns to its families through roundtables, webinars, and white papers.
