What Makes Family Capital Different

The priorities that set Family Capital apart from institutional capital, in terms families recognize, and how REALM answers each one.

Priority

For a family

How REALM answers

A patient, long-term horizon

Families invest for the generations that follow, well beyond any single hold period.

Hold periods shaped by each family’s objectives and each investment’s business plan.

Preservation before growth

Families protect what they have built before they reach for income and growth.

Underwriting through the REALM Factor Framework and asset-level diligence before each commitment.

Tax sensitivity

Families measure every return after tax, across trusts, entities, and generations.

Direct ownership, with depreciation, cost segregation, and 1031 exchanges structured around the family’s tax position.

Control

Families want a say in what they own and how their capital is put to work.

Strategies across the risk-return spectrum, with each family choosing its strategies and its level of participation.
