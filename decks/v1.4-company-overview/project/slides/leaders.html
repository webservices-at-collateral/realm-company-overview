Leadership Bios

REALM’s team has decades of experience successfully managing capital over multiple cycles, throughout the capital stack, and across property types, geographies and risk profiles.

Travis King

Founder and CEO

Travis King is the Founder and CEO of REALM, where he oversees the firm with a focus on culture, strategy, and investments. Before founding REALM, he spent nearly a decade as co-founder and CEO of Brixton Capital, a private investment firm. He has more than $3.7 billion of transactional experience over 25 years across industrial, office, multifamily, hospitality, and retail real estate. He graduated magna cum laude and Phi Beta Kappa from the University of California, San Diego, and holds an MBA from Harvard Business School.

Greg Drakos

Chief Operating Officer

Greg Drakos is Chief Operating Officer of REALM, where he leads the firm’s operations and infrastructure and works across its investment strategies. Before REALM, he was Managing Partner of Cross Real Estate Investors and spent nearly seven years at Carrington, first as a portfolio manager at Carrington Capital Management and later as Executive Vice President of the Special Situations Group at Carrington Holding Company. Earlier, he worked in leveraged finance investment banking at Merrill Lynch. He is a graduate of Cornell University.

Will Moyer

Principal

Will Moyer is a Principal at REALM, where he manages the firm’s external relationships with investor families and operating partners. Before REALM, he spent nearly nine years in a leadership role at Stand Together Chamber of Commerce. He holds a Bachelor of Arts in Political Science and Entrepreneurship from Grove City College.
