The REALM Advantage

1

Built intentionally for Family Capital

REALM was built by Family Capital, for Family Capital, and serves families first. Each structure is built around the family’s tax position, holding period objectives, and the generations that follow.

2

Family Capital at scale

The equity REALM’s families commit together lets REALM compete for middle-market transactions, finance at the portfolio level, and close with certainty, while each family still chooses its investments and reaches the team directly.

3

Proprietary investment opportunities

Opportunities come from operating partners, brokers, and the families themselves, often before a marketed sale, because owners and operators bring deals to capital that closes.

4

Ground-level market insight

Operating partners, local brokers, and families who own property across REALM’s markets see shifts in leasing and pricing before published reports. The REALM Factor Framework turns that insight into a read on each dislocation.

5

One relationship across the lifecycle

Strategy, sourcing, structuring, management, and the transfer to heirs sit with one team, so a family’s real estate is managed as a single portfolio with clear accountability from acquisition to transfer.

6

A network of like-minded peers

REALM brings its families together to share perspectives, knowledge, and values within the network and across the next generation, so what one family sees on the ground informs the next family’s decision.
